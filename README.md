## EventOS demo phases implemented
- Attendee intelligence: personalized dashboard, schedule, profile and grounded Copilot.
- Navigation intelligence: simplified MTW venue graph, A* routing, accessibility preference, crowd-aware costs and WebAR demo entry point.
- Smart food: capacity-controlled pickup slots, mock payment and pickup QR/order ID.
- Networking: synthetic opt-in people matching and MTW company discovery.
- Organizer command center: simulated occupancy, crowd pressure, food/networking metrics and deterministic surge demo.
- Safety workflow: medical, security, accessibility and lost/separated assistance flows.

Operational telemetry, food data, orders and attendee profiles are demo/synthetic data unless explicitly marked as official public MTW information. EventOS is not affiliated with or integrated into Mumbai Tech Week.
